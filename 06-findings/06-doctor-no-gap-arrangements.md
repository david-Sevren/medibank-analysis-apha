# Finding 6: Medibank's doctor no-gap arrangements and its part-owned hospitals

Desk research, 27 Nov 2026. Public sources only. Refs (D1 to D30) are in `04-sources/sources-log.md`.

## The question

Is Medibank offering doctors agreements (no out-of-pocket for members, presumably better pay for doctors) only when they operate at hospitals Medibank part-owns, especially for joint replacements? If such agreements exist, are they extended to other hospitals?

## Short answer

Medibank does run doctor arrangements that apply only at named hospitals: its **No Gap program**. A surgeon's no-gap rate applies only when the surgeon and the hospital both participate; at any other hospital the member pays a gap (D1, D2). So the hospital-specific mechanism Brett describes is real.

But it is **not limited to hospitals Medibank part-owns**. The program started in 2019 at Vermont Private (no Medibank stake). Medibank's own 2025 list names 36 hospitals, of which only three are Medibank-linked (Adeney, East Sydney Private, Macquarie University Hospital via The Orthopaedic Institute). It reached 41 sites by FY25 and added St John of God Murdoch in Sep 2025 (D3 to D8).

What is unique to a Medibank hospital is **Adeney**: the only hospital where Medibank's no-gap model covers every service, where every surgeon has to sign up to it, and where the rate is reported (not confirmed) at 92% of the AMA fee. Even there, **Bupa offers the same zero out-of-pocket model** (D13 to D16).

No public source shows Medibank paying doctors more at its own hospitals than at other No Gap hospitals. The contracts are confidential, so this can't be confirmed or ruled out from public information.

## What this means for Brett

**Can't be said:**
- "Medibank only offers no gap at hospitals it owns." Most No Gap hospitals have no Medibank stake.
- "Medibank pays doctors more at its own hospitals." There is no public evidence either way.

**Can be said:**
- Medibank's most generous no-gap model, covering every service and every surgeon, exists only at a hospital it part-owns (Adeney), with Bupa also participating.
- The doctors working under these models at Medibank's hospitals are often its co-owners.

**Worth pursuing:** whether the **terms** differ. Is the rate paid at Adeney, East Sydney and TOI higher than at Vermont, Hurstville or Lingard for the same procedure? Has Medibank declined to add hospitals near its own sites to the No Gap network? The 90% jump in No Gap use in HY26 came the year Adeney opened, but how much of the volume goes through Medibank-linked hospitals is not public.

## How to test it

1. **APHA members:** ask which hospitals applied to join Medibank's No Gap network and were declined or not answered, especially near Adeney, East Sydney and Macquarie (for example Wyvern, Northern Private, Monash Heart, Southern Sydney Private, which also lack hospital agreements; see Finding 3).
2. **Surgeons:** ask surgeons who operate at both a Medibank-linked hospital and another hospital whether Medibank offered No Gap at one and not the other, and at what rate. The AMA notes contracts are usually confidential, so this needs care.
3. **Bupa and HCF:** they also run no-gap models at Adeney, East Sydney and Macquarie. If their rates there match Medibank's, that weakens any preferential-pay argument. If Medibank's are higher only at its own hospitals, that is the finding.
4. **Medibank:** put the question directly. What share of No Gap admissions in FY26 were at Adeney, East Sydney and TOI? Is the Adeney schedule offered at any other hospital?
5. **Agreement data (ours):** check whether the non-owned No Gap hospitals all hold Medibank hospital agreements, and whether any No Gap hospital near a Medibank site has left the program. That needs the current participating list from Medibank.
