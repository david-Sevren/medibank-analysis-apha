## Priority (agreed with David, 24 Nov 2026)

Start with **contracting behaviour around Medibank-interest hospitals**: did Medibank's contracting with *other* hospitals in the same catchment change after it took an interest? This is where Medibank would have the clearest lever. It also links claims 3 and 4.
