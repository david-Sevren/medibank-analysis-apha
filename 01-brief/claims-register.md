## Priority (agreed with David, 24 Nov 2026)

Start with **contracting behaviour around Medibank-interest hospitals**: did Medibank's contracting with *other* hospitals in the same catchment change after it took an interest? This is where Medibank would have the clearest lever. It also links claims 3 and 4.

Step 1 is the ownership register (`02-ownership/`).

## Regional contracting test (27 Nov 2026)

No detectable pattern of Medibank dropping or withholding agreements near its part-owned hospitals. Rebuilt ESPH figure: 81.6% (Aug 2020) to 80.0% (Jul 2025) within 30 km, against APHA's withdrawn 54.89%. Details: `06-findings/01-regional-contracting.md`.
