**Of interest**
- **Not Medibank-exclusive.** At opening, newsGP reported Medibank and **Bupa** had both signed up to the no-gap model, with talks under way with other funds [H21]. If that's still true it cuts against Brett's claim 6 at this site. Need the current list of participating funds.
- **Doctors may be paid more there.** The Australian Society of Anaesthetists (May 2026 position statement, July 2026 letter to The Age) says Adeney runs an "uplift rebate schedule", reportedly at parity for surgeons and anaesthetists at **92% of the AMA rate for most procedures and 65% for endoscopy**, and "far more favourable to anaesthetists than the usual rebates" [H23]. If the insurer pays a richer medical schedule only at hospitals it part-owns, that is the concrete version of claim 6. **Priority lead.**
- The Age / SMH ran "There are no fees at this new private hospital. Some doctors hate it" on 1 July 2026 [H23]. Worth reading in full for surgeon testimony.
- CEO said doctors who are also shareholders must declare that when admitting [H21]. Self-referral by the 42-45 doctor-owners is a separate steering channel from Medibank's own conduct.
- Two event dates: JV announcement (Mar 2021) and opening (Feb/Mar 2025).

- Medibank's agreement is with MUH (Macquarie University), not with TOI. So TOI can't test "Medibank has no agreement with its own hospital". But it can test whether Medibank's contracting with *other* North Shore / Ryde orthopaedic hospitals changed after Sep 2022 or Jan 2024.

It exceeded year-one targets; 1,800 public referrals and 1,000+ surgeries by mid-2025 [H30, H31]. A big share of its volume is public, which dilutes any "filling with Medibank members" story here.
- It is the only private hospital in Adelaide's western suburbs [H9], so the "competitor catchment" is thin. A contracting analysis here may have very few comparison hospitals.
- Second-tier default benefit eligibility lapsed 14 Sep 2026 [H19]. That would matter for members of funds *without* an agreement there. Check whether it has been renewed.
- Medibank also runs SA's My Home Hospital and a Transition Care Service through Amplar, so it has a wider footprint in SA than this one hospital.
- Short "after" window (Nov 2024 onward).

## Cross-cutting leads to follow

1. **Rebuild the ESPH number properly.** The 2025 claim was about the right question with the wrong data.
2. **Adeney's medical rebate schedule** (92% / 65% of AMA) vs Medibank's standard GapCover schedule at non-owned hospitals. This is the most concrete "better terms for its own hospitals" lead so far.
3. **Which funds run no gap at each site.** Bupa at Adeney; HCF at MUH/TOI. If competitors' funds are in too, Medibank will say these hospitals are open to all.
4. **Doctor-owner self-referral** at Adeney, ESPH, TOI and Western is a separate channel from insurer steering. Keep the two apart.
5. **Public-patient share** at ESPH (~20%) and Western (state contract) affects any "filling with Medibank members" claim.
